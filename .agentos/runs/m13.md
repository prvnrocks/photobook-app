**Totals:** about 9 app taps (not counting login typing or the photo picker), 9 screens counting the email app, about 10 visible decisions, 2 dead waits, and a 6-step progress bar (`src/lib/app/flow-steps.ts`).

**Friction points:**
- **Choices that could be defaulted or inferred:**
  - Occasion is required with no default (`actions.ts:39`).
  - Tone, caption language, shape and style already have defaults but are still shown as decisions.
  - Shape could be guessed from photo orientation, language from the device or memory, and the title is already AI-suggested.
- **Fear copy:** "these two can't be changed later" (D-063) appears before the user has seen any photo.
- **Screens that could merge:** New book + Upload; Build + first preview; Review + Editor; Done + Publish.
- **Waits are wasted:**
  - Upload and build waits could collect the one question we need (occasion) and show the draft.
  - The rule-based draft album already exists after the upload finishes processing (`UploadSummary` "N pages in your draft album") but is never shown.
- **Jargon:** "Update album" vs "Rebuild with AI" (D-028 vs rebuild), "Kept/Dropped", "spread", "Page" vs "Pages" vs "Add page", "Publish changes", "Step 4 of 6".
- **Dead ends:**
  - A user-edited album makes review show only "Continue in the editor" (D-037 #2).
  - Login from the WhatsApp in-app browser needs an app switch and code entry.
- **Unnecessary taps:** "Build my album" is a forced tap almost everyone makes. "Done" then "Publish" is two steps for one intent.

## 2. Competitor benchmark (approximate, from memory, no web check)

| App | What it asks | What it does automatically | Time to first preview |
|---|---|---|---|
| Popsa | Photos only (it suggests sets from the camera roll); theme and size later; account at checkout | Layout on the device, then theme and text | About 5–15 s |
| Chatbooks | Account first, then photos (or Instagram) | Fills the book, one photo per page plus captions | About 30 s |
| Mixbook | Theme and size first, then upload; "Smart create" is optional | Autofill, desktop-centred | 1–3 min, many decisions |
| Shutterfly | Product, size, theme, then upload | Autofill, or "Make My Book" (human-made, days) | Minutes |
| Google Photos books (discontinued in many regions) | Nothing: books were suggested from the library | Picks and lays out about 20 pages | Instant |
| Apple Memories | Nothing | Picks, groups, adds titles; editable | Instant (not a book) |

**Pattern:** the fastest apps ask for photos first and nothing else, show a finished draft in seconds, ask about theme or format after the preview, and ask for an account last. We can beat them by doing that plus AI captions, Arabic and WhatsApp sharing.

## 3. Target flow

**Goal:** after choosing photos, at most 3 app taps to a shared WhatsApp link. First preview in under 60 s from the end of photo selection with 40 photos (measured in e2e). Shared link in under 3 min with AI live, or under 90 s with AI in replay mode.

1. **Start with photos.**
   - The landing CTA becomes "Make your book" → login (first time only) → `/books/start`, a single big "Choose photos" button.
   - On the dashboard, the "New book" button opens the phone's picker directly (the button is the file input's label).
   - After selection, a server action creates a draft book with defaults, and the selected files are handed to `Uploader` in memory through client-side navigation.
   - Why not open the picker after navigating: iOS only opens the picker from a direct user tap, so it can't be auto-opened after a page change.
2. **Set up while uploading.** The upload screen shows progress, a growing thumbnail grid and one optional card: "What's this book for?" (6 chips, default Other).
   - A "Change" disclosure holds feel (follows the occasion), language (from memory or device locale), title (blank means AI suggests one), and shape (suggested from photo orientation).
   - It also holds style, which follows the occasion (D-068).
   - This is the single editable summary.
3. **The build starts by itself** when the upload settles (first build only, at least 5 photos ready). There is no "Build my album" tap.
4. **Show the book while it builds.** The build screen shows the rule-based draft as a read-only page strip or flip preview, with "AI is choosing your best photos and writing captions…". When the run finishes, the user goes **straight to the editor**.
5. **Review folds into the editor.** A chip at the top of the editor says "AI kept 34 of 40 · See 6 left out". It opens the existing review route as a secondary screen, so its logic is unchanged.
   - Memory Keeper still runs at the editor's finish step (`edit/actions.ts`), so D-032 is kept.
6. **Simple editor by default.**
   - At most 3 large tools per selection: Page = Layout / Background / More; Photo = Replace / Crop / More; Caption = Edit / AI / More.
   - Everything else sits behind "More".
   - The header's primary button becomes **"Share"** instead of "Done".
7. **Share is one screen.** "Share" in the editor saves, publishes (the button's subtitle says "Free link lasts 30 days") and lands on a share screen where the link is ready.
   - On that screen: WhatsApp (native link), Copy, Share sheet, then the free vs paid card and Upgrade/PDF below.
   - The flow is 2 taps (Share, WhatsApp). One tap is not reliable on iOS because a share after an awaited publish loses the tap's permission.
8. The progress bar goes from 6 steps to 3: **Photos → Your book → Share**.

**Guest mode (try before signing in):**
- **How it would work:** feasible with Supabase anonymous sign-ins. They give a real `auth.uid()`, so RLS and storage policies keep working unchanged.
- **Costs and risks:**
  - Every policy granted to `authenticated` then also covers guests (needs an audit plus rls_check updates).
  - Unlimited throwaway uids defeat the per-user daily AI cap (D-026). That needs Turnstile CAPTCHA, a per-IP cap and the daily budget breaker (D-059).
  - Unclaimed guest photos must be deleted (7-day cron sweep).
  - Claiming the account at Share needs identity linking. If the email already has an account, we need a server-side **merge of books** from the guest uid. That is the real complexity and a privacy risk.
  - Publish, checkout and PDF must refuse `is_anonymous` users on the server.
- **Recommendation:** treat it as M13b, decided after measuring login drop-off with M13a analytics.

## Decisions for you

1. **Change D-063 to "fixed once the AI build starts" instead of "fixed at creation".** This allows photos-first with a shape and style that can still be changed on the setup card. Recommend: **yes**. Without it, a 1-screen "pick style" step stays before the photos.
2. **Skip the review screen by default** (build goes to the editor; review is reached from the chip). Recommend: **yes**. AI choices stay visible and editable, so "AI drafts, user decides" still holds.
3. **Start the first AI build automatically after upload** (first build only, at least 5 photos, normal caps). Recommend: **yes**.
4. **Guest mode / try before signing in.** Recommend: **not in M13**. Measure login drop-off with M13 analytics first; if it's large, do M13b with anonymous sign-ins, CAPTCHA, a per-IP AI cap, a 7-day sweep of unclaimed guest data, and account merge.
5. **"Share" publishes in one tap** (30-day free notice on the button; it also publishes later edits). Recommend: **yes**, keeping the WhatsApp tap separate for iOS reliability.
